## Decision Context

Sprint 17 established a reproducible short-budget baseline and bounded ablation result, but every trainable arm stopped at 300 optimizer steps, about 7.5 epochs on its Fit support. The recorded contrastive coefficient remained exactly zero for every training trace because the 980-step warmup exceeded the complete run. Sprint 17 therefore compared prediction-trained representations and train-free scorer variants; it did not test the intended joint prediction-plus-contrastive regime.

Sprint 17 also produced non-trivial relative leads for K3, K7, and K9 on Confirmation. None passed the frozen recovery conjunction, and unaffected-background stability failed for B0 and every comparison arm on Confirmation. Sprint 18 treats those leads as hypotheses worth retesting, not as established winners.

The user additionally authorized four new directions while preserving the project mindset: healthy-only self-supervision, EMA latent prediction, no waveform reconstruction, Conditional BatchNorm, independent `S_pred` and `S_pop`, and post-hoc-only anomaly labels.
