# Prospective exploratory benchmark with bounded soft support gates — proposal

**Status:** Design proposal only; **not an executable protocol**, amendment to Sprint 18, change to [Benchmark Measurability Exit Gates V2](BENCHMARK_MEASURABILITY_EXIT_GATES_V2.md), or authorization to generate data or train. The approved Sprint 18 construction quota of 48 selected controls, its verdicts, blocked model tasks, and the stop before Cycle 4 remain unchanged. A separately approved versioned protocol and evidence review are required before any new role/seed contact.

## Question and permitted claim

First ask whether a healthy-only representation and its independent `S_pred`/`S_pop` scores retain a measurable signal in a **controlled synthetic setting**. This is an exploratory question about model behavior conditional on the declared simulation and its available support. It is not evidence of real-world prevalence, calibrated deployment risk, generalization to unseen fleets, or independently confirmed early-warning performance. A later confirmatory benchmark must use a separately frozen, untouched set of whole histories and a prespecified uncertainty/acceptance analysis. Exploratory histories cannot become that Confirmation set.

[Task 64's audit](../artifacts/sprint-18/task-64.md) establishes that 48 is a binding **construction** reserve, while 32 is the Design promotion target (`ceil(25 × 1.25)`) and 25 is the inherited hard floor for selected negative-control windows. Construction and qualification count the same deterministic window list on identical history inputs; windows are not independent histories. The audit proves neither that 48 is statistically necessary nor that 32/25 is a safe construction replacement. Existing research blocks (14/16 on each profile over 16 seeds, and 16/16 on a separate eight-seed block) cannot be pooled or used to select favorable seeds. This proposal does not reclassify any of those rows.

## Proposed exploratory soft rule (not a Sprint 18 gate)

The independent accounting unit is **one complete evaluation history**, not a file, window, event, repeated profile on the same seed, or the number of checks in a report. For a future exploratory protocol, the proposed fixed evaluation roster size is **32 disjoint whole histories**. This is an engineering-sized example of “a few dozen,” **not** a power calculation or an assertion of population pass probability. The final role/seed binding and sample-size justification require prospective review before any data contact; if that review chooses another size or budget, publish a new version before outcomes instead of resizing this one afterward.

**Hard across all roles:** frozen causal/provenance/identity/seed isolation, no leakage, valid chronology and maintenance/reset/censoring semantics, and complete computable evidence for each role's declared use. **Hard for each exploratory evaluation history:** usable event/control and window definitions; inherited per-history positive/control/robot-day floors (`P≥10`, `W≥10`, `A≥8`, total≥30, negative controls≥25, robot-days≥150), six positive and six negative contributing robots, two programs in each P/W cohort, P/W lead support≥80%, and the robot/program concentration caps. **Fit and Calibration are healthy-only roles with their own frozen support and isolation requirements, not positive-event floors.** Missing or uncomputable evidence for a required check is a hard failure, not a soft miss. There is no soft allowance for source/version mismatches, role leakage, invalid causality, missing whole histories, or Confirmation.

**Candidate soft checks, evaluated only after every hard check passes:**

- Each *Design-style margin* rather than its hard floor: `P≥13`, `W≥13`, `A≥10`, total positives≥38, controls≥32, robot-days≥188. A shortfall counts as **one miss for each named predicate**; meeting the corresponding hard floor remains mandatory. The current 48 construction rule does not thereby become 32: the future exploratory protocol would have to specify and justify its own construction rule prospectively.
- Full eligible-set cohort mix relative to the existing 15–60% target may count as **one miss per affected cohort** only inside a proposed exploratory absolute guard band of **12.5–62.5%**; outside that band is a hard failure. This band is an explicit, provisional engineering tolerance of ±2.5 percentage points around the existing limits, **not** a statistically derived safe band. It is motivated by the question of tolerating small departures, not by permission to rejudge old histories. A future protocol must justify this band against its estimand and report its effects on event support before approval; otherwise remove this soft category rather than silently expanding it.

**Budget and verdict:** at most **two soft predicate×history misses total**, occurring in at most **two distinct histories**, with **no more than one miss in either history**, across all 32 frozen evaluation histories. Correlated failures of multiple checks on the same history count separately and exceed that history's allowance; counting “two out of dozens of checks” without an immutable denominator is forbidden. Every declared history and every predicate remains in the report, including hard failures; do not replace, retry, pool away, or discard a miss. Zero hard failures and zero soft misses permit only `EXPLORATORY_USABLE`; zero hard failures and one or two allowed soft misses permit only `EXPLORATORY_USABLE_WITH_QUALIFICATIONS`. Any hard failure, third soft miss, second soft miss on one history, missing history, or missing check means `EXPLORATORY_NOT_SUITABLE` for the planned comparison. Even `EXPLORATORY_USABLE` is not an EG2/EG0–EG6 `MEASURABLE` verdict.

For example, one history with controls 29 (above the hard floor 25 but below the Design target 32) and another with eligible A share 14.8% (inside the proposed exploratory guard band, with all event floors met) would use the two soft misses **only on a new roster frozen under this rule**. Two margin shortfalls on one history would fail this proposed rule; so would 24 controls on any history. The historical Sprint 18 Design history with A=20/135=14.81% retains its original failed verdict regardless of this example.

Report metrics for **all 32 histories** with history-blocked uncertainty, plus explicit support-stratified diagnostics labeled secondary. Never publish only the 30 best-supported histories as the primary result. Record the exact failed predicate, margin, cohort, seed/role identity, observable support and impact on each estimand; an unusable history cannot quietly disappear from denominators. Any per-arm comparison must use the same prespecified roster and support; no model score can determine which history gets a soft allowance.

## Why soft is not synonymous with safe

One or two misses among 32 histories do **not** imply a known failure rate or a confidence guarantee, especially with correlated predicates and simulator-specific seeds. The proposed `32`, `2`, and mix guard band are design choices for an exploratory exercise, not inferential thresholds validated by existing data. The stricter per-history floors, causal windows, unaffected controls, robot/program spread, label isolation and honest reporting still protect interpretability. If model inference requires a missed support check to define an estimand (for example, an insufficient cohort makes its median/lead statistic uncomputable), that endpoint must be marked unavailable on that history even when the overall exploratory dataset receives a qualified-use label. Do not promote a qualified exploratory label to independent Confirmation or operational readiness.

**Subsequent Sprint 19 note (not part of the original proposal):** Sprint 19 Task 2's [threshold rationale](exploratory-benchmark-threshold-rationale.md) and [protocol draft](exploratory-benchmark-protocol-draft-v1.md) exclude the illustrative `12.5–62.5%` band from any operative option because portions outside the inherited hard `15–60%` cohort-mix limits conflict with that hard predicate. The original band above is retained as historical proposal text, not a live option or a retroactive change to this proposal.

## Before an executable protocol can be approved

A separately authorized protocol must bind exact Fit, Calibration and 32 evaluation history identities, disjoint seeds/roots and source digests; specify the exploratory construction quota and why its selected windows support the chosen score/false-alert estimands; justify the 12.5–62.5% guard band or remove it; define all primary endpoints and history-blocked uncertainty; and fix hard/soft checker outputs, error handling and precedence before outcome contact. It must be reviewed independently and cannot inherit Sprint 18's `MEASURABLE` label or re-use its retired/Sealed or sandbox seeds. A later confirmatory protocol needs its own untouched roles, scientifically justified power/support and per-history gates; any change to inherited EG gates must be explicit and prospectively versioned.

**No execution authorized by this proposal:** no generator/preflight run, threshold/code change, Cycle 4, Sprint 18 rescue, model training, or Confirmation/Sealed inspection. [Sprint 19](sprint-plans/sprint-19.md) is a separate design-only review of the proposed estimands, allowances, and support assumptions; it cannot bind seeds/roles or authorize new data contact. Graphify is not part of this workflow per user instruction.
