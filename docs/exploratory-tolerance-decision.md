# Exploratory Tolerance Decision: NOT_READY

**Status: DESIGN ONLY / NOT EXECUTABLE / NO DATA CONTACT.** This memo issues a bounded prospective recommendation from the reviewed Sprint 20 Task 1 analysis and Sprint 19 design record. It changes no Gate V2 predicate, Sprint 18 construction rule or verdict, and grants no authority to bind roles, contact histories, generate data, score, train, inspect Confirmation/Sealed material, or continue Sprint 18.

## Decision

**Recommendation: `NOT_READY` for any positive numerical support-margin allowance or tolerance-based acceptance rule.** Keep the current no-new-allowance position. The analysis can describe hard-pass histories that miss a Design margin, but it does not establish that an allowance is safe, statistically validated, or useful for an approved exploratory-use decision. In particular, the proposed `H=32` roster and `m=2` miss budget are illustrative, not justified operating thresholds. The conditional bounds quantify possible exposure; they do not supply a materiality criterion, empirical variance, or probability of qualification.

The restricted `A≥10` / `robot-days≥188` candidate is a narrower question than tolerating shortfalls in all six Design-margin categories, because `A` and robot-days do not directly enter the primary `P+W` AUROC formula when its P/W event and control support is held fixed. That distinction is not a safety result: `A` is a secondary event endpoint and part of the all-positive mix denominator; robot-days support any separately frozen false-alert-per-robot-day interpretation. Either can co-vary with total positives, controls, mix, eligibility, or concentration. No evidence supports declaring their shortfalls immaterial to every outcome.

The original all-six proposal with `H=32, m=2` exposes histories to shortfalls in direct primary-support categories (`P`, `W`, total positives, controls) as well as `A` and robot-days. The numerical budget does not compare their different effects, resolve correlated flags, or validate an acceptance decision. Neither candidate is approved. The 12.5–62.5% proposal mix band is excluded: the inherited per-history `15–60%` mix predicate remains hard.

“No allowance” here means **no new soft-pass disposition**. It does not change Gate V2's distinction between hard floors and Design promotion margins, nor does it make a hard-pass, margin-shortfall endpoint disappear from descriptive full-roster reporting. Gate V2 `MEASURABLE`, an exploratory qualified-use label, real-world deployment claims, and independent Confirmation are separate claim levels; this memo authorizes none of them.

These six values are not six independent checks. If eligible positives partition into `P`, `W`, and `A`, total positives are their sum; `P`/`W` shortfalls can therefore also trigger the total-positive flag, and an `A` shortfall can do so when other counts are held fixed. Cohort mix is computed from the positive counts and is coupled to them. Controls, robot-days, event/control eligibility, and robot/program concentration can also co-vary.

The table gives no soft range for cohort mix. Each of `P`, `W`, and `A` must remain within the inherited `15–60%` of all eligible positives in every history. Neither the historical `12.5–62.5%` example nor any other mix excursion is eligible to be counted as a soft miss here. Minimum contributing robots and programs, concentration limits, lead support, causal/chronology/eligibility rules, provenance, common score support, identity and role isolation, and every other inherited hard condition also remain mandatory.

## What the two-miss arithmetic does—and does not—say

Task 1 defines `m` as the number of **distinct complete histories** carrying at least one margin-only miss, not the number of predicate flags. For the original proposal's illustrative `H=32, m=2` case, its cap means at most two predicate-by-history flags total, on two distinct histories, and no more than one flag on either history. This is an immutable-roster denominator in the illustration, not a validated budget. The restricted `A`/robot-day candidate has no selected `H` or miss/history budget; neither value is inherited from the original proposal. Applying `32/2` to it would remain hypothetical and would not establish that either category can be tolerated.

For each primary branch, `A_{s,h}∈[0,1]`. The missed-history contribution can range from `[0, m/H]`; replacing those endpoints can move a one-branch full macro by at most `m/H`. At the illustrative values this is `[0, 2/32]`, with a maximum shift of `2/32 = 0.0625` (6.25 percentage points). This is a worst-case range, not an observed AUROC change: a support label by itself changes no endpoint, and counts alone do not give a realized effect.

For a **same-branch, paired arm contrast**, each per-history difference is in `[-1,1]`. The two missed histories' contribution can range from `[-2/32, +2/32]`; its full width, and the maximum contrast change from replacing both paired terms, is `4/32 = 0.125` (12.5 percentage points). This is not a comparison of raw `S_pred` and `S_pop` score scales, not the difference between those two primary branch macros, and not an arm contrast unless a future protocol prospectively defines paired arms.

Task 1 also gives only an **assumption-conditional, two-sided marginal 95% Hoeffding illustration** at `H=32`: approximately `0.2401` AUROC units for one branch bounded in `[0,1]`, and `0.4802` contrast units for a paired history-level difference bounded in `[-1,1]`. These radii require iid complete histories from a named target population, fixed pre-outcome rules, and a computable endpoint on every sampled history; pairing is within each history, not independent arms. They are very loose and are not simultaneous coverage for both branches or a family of arms. They do not establish a useful precision level, an iid target or sampling plan, or the safety of a margin rule. The paired `S_pred` and `S_pop` outcomes use the same histories and may be dependent; no branch independence is assumed.

AUROC pair terms are not independent observations: event scores recur across controls and control scores recur across events. Counts such as `|E_h||C_h|` do not replace the whole-history unit or provide an empirical score-variance model. The available analysis has no observed score distributions or score-level variance, no between-history endpoint variance, no support-flag frequency or joint dependence estimate, and no empirically justified materiality threshold. The deterministic whole-history bootstrap convention (`B=2000`, RNG seed `20260202`) is a descriptive resampling summary, not a substitute for those missing assumptions or a precision guarantee.

For the restricted candidate, an `A` miss has no direct `P+W` pair-count effect only if P/W counts and eligible controls are held fixed. It still changes or weakens support for the secondary `A` endpoint and can alter the all-positive mix denominator; a resulting mix violation is a hard failure, not a soft A miss. A robot-day miss does not enter threshold-free AUROC directly, but limits exposure for a false-alert-per-robot-day diagnostic if that diagnostic is separately frozen. It says nothing about the independence of histories, windows, or AUROC pair terms. Therefore an A/day-only miss cannot be dismissed as irrelevant to every outcome or claim.

## Full-roster and secondary-stratum evidence required in any future design

Any later, separately authorized protocol must freeze score-independent support flags before outcomes and report in this order:

1. **Full roster first.** For every declared history, record role/identity under the future contract, each hard predicate, each candidate margin, exact support and shortfall, and separate finite `S_pred` and `S_pop` per-history `P+W` AUROCs. Report both complete-roster macros separately. A hard structural failure or a missing/non-finite required endpoint makes the full-roster primary result `UNCOMPUTABLE`; so does a branch-support mismatch. Do not drop a history, compute a better-supported-subset primary, assign a substitute score, or bootstrap only computable histories. A margin-only hard-pass miss does not itself change a computed AUROC.
2. **Secondary support strata.** For each branch, show counts and per-history endpoint distributions for histories meeting all descriptive margins, each named miss predicate (including separate `A` and robot-day strata), and the union of distinct histories with any miss. Include P-only, W-only, and A secondary endpoint values/support, and any separately frozen false-alert-per-robot-day diagnostic with its exposure. Mark empty or uncomputable strata explicitly. Report overlapping predicate flags and the distinct-history union separately; do not sum flags as independent histories.
3. **Paired arm sensitivity, only if prospectively declared.** Show every same-branch per-history paired difference, full-roster contrast, and the same strata on identical paired history membership. A missing paired endpoint makes that full-roster contrast uncomputable; no complete-case contrast replaces it.
4. **Bounded-influence accounting.** State the exact predicate-defined set of `m` distinct histories and its `m/H` macro and `2m/H` paired-contrast arithmetic. Describe full-roster versus stratum contrasts as descriptive sensitivity, never proof of equivalence or causation. The margin-met-only result is never the primary endpoint.

No materiality cutoff or endpoint-sensitivity acceptance threshold is supplied here. Any future policy must name its intended interpretation and a justified, prospectively fixed criterion before outcome contact. A sign reversal, a difference beyond that criterion, an unstable P-only versus W-only pattern, or repeated co-occurrence of flags would be a sensitivity warning under that future rule—not permission to choose a more favorable subset. Similar strata would not prove equivalence or representativeness.

## Hard boundaries, construction, and stop/abort rules

- Keep every inherited per-history hard floor: `P≥10`, `W≥10`, `A≥8`, total positives `≥30`, controls `≥25`, and robot-days `≥150`. Keep the `15–60%` P/W/A mix, six positive-contributing and six control-contributing robots, two programs in each P and W cohort, no robot contributing more than 35% of positives or 40% of controls, no program contributing more than 60% of either predictable cohort, at least 80% qualifying lead support for each of P and W under the frozen causal-window and clean-baseline rules, and all chronology, eligibility, censoring/reset/maintenance/quarantine, common-support, finite-score, provenance, identity, and role-isolation requirements.
- Fit stays verified-healthy only; Calibration stays disjoint and healthy-only for its permitted uses; Evaluation is a complete, predeclared, disjoint whole-history role. Roles must be isolated by history/root/seed as required. This memo binds no roles, histories, or seeds and makes no threshold, score, or model decision.
- Retain the inherited downstream support references: Fit has at least 200 verified-healthy files and 6,000 valid patches in aggregate, with fallback/sparse groups reported; Calibration has at least 40 verified-healthy eligible rows. Confirmation, Sealed, quarantined, censored, and maintenance-overlapping rows stay out of Fit and Calibration. These inherited references do not establish their adequacy for a future representation-specific implementation.
- A value below a hard floor, a mix outside `15–60%`, any other hard-predicate failure, missing history/check, non-finite score, or branch-support mismatch is not a soft miss. Record `STRUCTURAL-FAIL` / `UNCOMPUTABLE` as applicable, and stop the planned comparison; never pool it away or relabel it.
- No retry, replacement history, seed/profile substitution, favorable-history deletion, post-outcome threshold change, or retrospective support reclassification. Missing or unusable endpoints cannot be rescued by an allowance. Do not contact new histories under this memo.
- Sprint 18's `48` selected negative-control-window construction reserve and its stop before Cycle 4 remain unchanged. The inherited `32` Design target and `25` hard floor are not proven safe substitutes for that construction reserve. A distinct future exploratory construction quota remains unresolved and needs its own prospective support rationale; this memo does not set one.
- The current stop condition is categorical: **no tolerance-based acceptance or execution is available.** A future policy that fails its predeclared hard conditions or its separately justified, fixed allowance/sensitivity criteria must be recorded as unsuitable/uncomputable under that future contract, with no rerun or replacement. The original proposal's two-miss verdict vocabulary is not activated by this memo.

## Concrete prerequisites to move beyond `NOT_READY`

These are attainable design/evidence deliverables, not analysis authorized by this memo:

1. **Name the target and claim.** Specify the synthetic mechanism and intended exploratory interpretation, whole-history target population/sampling process, whether the goal is description or a decision, and a scientifically chosen precision/materiality objective for each primary branch and any paired contrast. Decide prospectively how the two primary branches are handled together; do not invent a numeric cut or simultaneous-coverage claim from the current records.
2. **Justify `H` and any `m`/predicate budget.** Supply a history-level sampling/variance rationale and pre-outcome sensitivity analysis that states the exact denominator, whether a miss means a predicate-by-history flag or distinct history, treatment of overlapping flags, and why each category is covered. Use complete histories as units. If evidence requires an authorized independent pilot or simulation, obtain a separate explicit authorization and freeze its roles, target, rules, and analysis before contact; do not mine old Sprint 18 outcomes or tune to observed results.
3. **Measure score/support dependence at the right level.** Under that authorized prospective design, obtain per-history distributions and uncertainty for both branches, same-branch paired contrasts if relevant, joint support-flag patterns, and margin-stratum sensitivity. Model the shared P/W/total/mix relationships and any A/control/robot-day co-failures; never treat windows or pair terms as independent histories. A and robot-day strata must also show whether retained secondary claims remain computable and interpretable.
4. **Resolve construction support.** Separately justify the future exploratory control construction quota against the frozen primary and retained secondary estimands and their support. Preserve Sprint 18's 48-control reserve and historical verdicts; neither 32 nor 25 is an approved construction replacement.
5. **Freeze the whole contract and approvals.** Version the predicate registry, any proposed soft rule, materiality/sensitivity criteria, full-roster reports, status meanings, failure precedence, no-retry rules, role topology, exact provenance and required score/missing-data behavior; obtain independent prospective review with no actionable findings. Neither approval authorizes role binding or outcome contact unless the later approved contract explicitly does so. Any substantive change after review requires a new prospective version and review before contact.

No old candidate or Sprint 18 failure may be rescued, re-scored, reclassified, or used to choose H, m, a construction quota, a margin, or a materiality threshold. Passing this design recommendation review would close only the design decision; it would not make a protocol executable.
