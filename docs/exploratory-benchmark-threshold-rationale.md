# Exploratory Benchmark Threshold Rationale

**Status: DESIGN ONLY / NOT EXECUTABLE.** This note analytically assesses illustrative support allowances against the reviewed [Task 1 estimands](exploratory-benchmark-estimands.md). It changes no gate, protocol, quota, or historical verdict; authorizes no generator, preflight, model, seed, role, or outcome contact; and does not amend Gate V2 or Sprint 18.

## Decision summary

The present records justify **no new soft-pass allowance** for the illustrative `H=32`, two-miss budget, or Design-margin shortfalls. Those values can be used only to describe a hypothetical, pre-outcome sensitivity scenario; they are not a sample-size justification, precision guarantee, known pass probability, or acceptance rule. The proposed `12.5–62.5%` mix band must be omitted as a soft category under the unchanged Gate V2 contract: the only portions it newly permits, `12.5–<15%` and `>60–62.5%`, violate Gate V2's per-history hard `15–60%` mix constraint. Task 3 should not operationalize any of these illustrative allowances without a separately authorized, prospectively justified and reviewed protocol version.

Keep every inherited hard predicate. A history below any hard floor or failing another hard predicate is `STRUCTURAL-FAIL`; under Task 1 its primary endpoint is `UNCOMPUTABLE`. Do not substitute an AUROC, omit that history, or compute a primary macro/bootstrap over a better-supported subset. A computed endpoint from a history that meets hard support but falls short of a Design-style margin remains reportable on the full roster; its eventual disposition is not justified by the current miss budget.

The exploratory construction quota is **unresolved**. Sprint 18's 48-control construction reserve remains operative for Sprint 18; this note does not recommend reducing it to 32, 25, or another value.

## Estimand and support being evaluated

Task 1 defines two separate primary estimates, one for each independent score (`S_pred` and `S_pop`). On the same score-independent eligible support, each history contributes a tie-aware AUROC comparing its eligible `P+W` event-window scores with same-history control-window scores; event and control windows use the specified maximum-file aggregation. Each branch's primary estimate is the unweighted mean of the per-history AUROCs over the complete declared Evaluation roster. Histories, not files, events, windows, or pairwise comparisons, are the independent accounting and resampling units. The two branches share history keys and bootstrap indices but remain separate endpoints.

Task 1 specifies the full-roster rule: all histories must meet inherited hard support and both branches must have finite scores on identical support. A hard structural failure, missing/non-finite required score, branch support mismatch, or uncomputable per-history endpoint makes the paired primary result `UNCOMPUTABLE`; no history may be dropped to recover a numeric macro. A hard-pass history that misses a proposed Design-style margin can still contribute if its primary endpoint is computable. This note assesses whether that latter miss can be turned into a soft disposition; it does not alter Task 1's endpoint.

The inherited per-history hard support also includes `P≥10`, `W≥10`, `A≥8`, total positives `≥30`, controls `≥25`, and robot-days `≥150`; at least six positive-contributing and six negative-control-contributing robots; at least two represented programs in each of `P` and `W`; each of `P`, `W`, and `A` comprising 15–60% of eligible positives; no robot contributing more than 35% of positives or 40% of controls; and no program contributing more than 60% of either predictable cohort. At least 80% of `P` and 80% of `W` events in each history must meet the frozen lead-support conditions (three eligible endpoints in the causal seven-day horizon, one ending at least 24 hours before failure, no maintenance/recommissioning reset in-window, and a clean baseline). Provenance, causal eligibility, chronology, role isolation, and complete computable evidence remain hard constraints as well.

Fit and Calibration remain disjoint healthy-only roles, not substitutes for Evaluation event support. Task 1 records the inherited Gate V2 downstream references of at least 200 verified-healthy Fit files and 6,000 valid patches in aggregate, with every fallback or sparse conditioning group reported, and at least 40 eligible healthy Calibration rows. Confirmation, Sealed, quarantined, censored, and maintenance-overlapping rows are barred from Fit and Calibration. These are precedents, not proof that the thresholds are adequate for a new representation; representation-specific support remains unresolved.

The estimate is conditional on the declared synthetic mechanism and eligible support. It is not fleet prevalence, deployment-risk calibration, or independent confirmation. The threshold-free AUROC endpoint does not use Calibration to select a primary threshold; any thresholded diagnostics remain separately specified and secondary.

## Analytic sensitivity for `H=32` and two misses

For a hypothetical roster of 32 complete histories, each computed per-history AUROC receives weight `1/32 = 3.125%` in each branch's macro. If two distinct histories each have one permitted soft miss, together they carry `2/32 = 1/16 = 6.25%` of that macro. Since AUROC lies in `[0,1]`, the two values' combined possible contribution ranges from 0 to `2/32`; changing both from 0 to 1 changes the macro by at most 0.0625. This is a deterministic weight bound, not an estimate of their actual scores, the effect of a support deficit, the bootstrap interval width, or a safe tolerance.

One feasible joint hard-floor count vector is `P=10`, `W=10`, `A=10` (total positives `=30`), with `C=25`; its `P+W` AUROC contains `(20 × 25) = 500` event-control comparison terms. A vector meeting the P/W/control Design margins is `P=13`, `W=13`, `A=12` (total positives `=38`), with `C=32`; it contains `(26 × 32) = 832` terms. These are arithmetic terms in the AUROC, **not** 500 or 832 independent observations: each event score is reused against multiple controls, each control score against multiple events, and windows can share history/robot structure. The count alone yields no standard error, effective sample size, or precision claim. More comparison terms do not mean proportionally more independent information.

Under the Task 1 history bootstrap, a 32-history resample draws 32 whole histories with replacement. Conditional on exactly two specified histories carrying the soft labels, the expected number of draws of those histories is 2; the chance that neither appears in one replicate is `(30/32)^32 ≈ 0.1268`, and a specified one is absent with probability `(31/32)^32 ≈ 0.3621`. These are bootstrap resampling probabilities for the fixed hypothetical roster only—not the chance of a future history missing support, a pass rate, or evidence that two misses are harmless. The `B=2000` replicates, RNG seed `20260202`, and 2.5th-percentile `LCB95` specified by Task 1 describe computation of an uncertainty summary; they do not add histories or establish a sampling regime, coverage, or precision. The two branch results remain paired through identical history-index resamples, not independent replications.

The two-distinct-history restriction limits a hypothetical miss's macro weight and prevents two counted predicates from being concentrated in one history. It does not make predicates independent: total-positive, cohort-count, mix, robot-day, and control support can co-vary within a history. Nor does it ensure that histories with misses are representative of an unseen-history population. Multiple deficits on the same history must be counted as separate predicate×history misses; under the proposal's one-miss-per-history wording they exceed that history's allowance, even if all structural hard floors still pass.

If either of the two histories instead fails a hard predicate, it is not a soft miss. The paired primary macro and its bootstrap are `UNCOMPUTABLE` under Task 1, even if the other 30 histories have finite values. Publishing those 30 alone would change the estimand and violate full-roster accounting.

## Per-predicate margin implications

The candidate Design-style numbers and inherited hard floors are summarized below. The first five margins are structural support thresholds, not precision estimates. A margin-only deficit means the value falls below the Design target while remaining at or above the hard floor; all other hard predicates still apply.

| Candidate predicate | Hard floor → Design margin | Margin-only range / gap | Relationship to the Task 1 primary endpoint |
|---|---:|---|---|
| Progressive events (`P`) | `10 → 13` | A hard-pass value of `10–12` misses the margin by 3, 2, or 1 events. | Directly supplies part of the `P+W` event set for both AUROCs. Less P support can make that within-history ranking less stable; it remains computable only if all hard, lead, eligibility, and score requirements pass. |
| Weak-precursor events (`W`) | `10 → 13` | A hard-pass value of `10–12` misses by 3, 2, or 1 events. | Directly supplies the other part of `P+W`; the same support and computability caveat applies. |
| Abrupt events (`A`) | `8 → 10` | A hard-pass value of `8–9` misses by 2 or 1 events. | `A` is not in the primary `P+W` event numerator; it is a secondary endpoint and contributes to the full eligible-event mix denominator. It cannot be treated as a primary predictive requirement. |
| Total positive events | `30 → 38` | A hard-pass value of `30–37` is 8 through 1 below the margin. | Affects overall event support and may overlap with P/W/A margin deficits; if P/W event counts are the source of the shortfall, it also bears on primary support. It is not an independent check. |
| Negative-control windows | `25 → 32` | A hard-pass count of `25–31` is 7 through 1 below the margin. | Directly supplies the control side of both per-history AUROCs. Window counts are not independent histories or independent pairwise comparisons. |
| Evaluable robot-days | `150 → 188` | The margin is 38 robot-days above the floor; any hard-pass value below 188 misses it. | Does not enter the threshold-free AUROC formula directly. It supports exposure accounting and the secondary false-alert-per-robot-day diagnostic if that diagnostic is separately frozen. |

In the primary AUROC, the P and W event windows are combined, so their relative counts also affect the within-history P/W composition. The between-history macro gives every history equal weight; it does not make within-history event windows or controls independent. A shortfall in `A` or robot-days does not directly reduce the primary pair count, but those checks still have their own inherited hard floors and can affect secondary interpretation. `P`, `W`, `A`, and total-positive margins are overlapping summaries: for example, a total-positive miss can coexist with all three cohort counts meeting their individual margins. Counting each named miss separately is essential; it cannot be converted into an undifferentiated “two of many checks” score.

The proposed soft rule preserves the above hard floors but does not show why any particular number of such margin misses is tolerable, whether all predicates have comparable impact, or how score distributions and history-level heterogeneity affect the bootstrap. The existing Design margins are mandatory promotion criteria under Gate V2, not optional soft gates there. Task 1 allows a computable hard-pass margin miss to remain in the estimand, but explicitly leaves its later disposition to this decision. On the current evidence, the margins are defensible as **per-history descriptive support annotations and predeclared sensitivity strata only**, not as an accepted soft-pass budget or `EXPLORATORY_USABLE[_WITH_QUALIFICATIONS]` rule.

## Mix-band conflict and hard-failure boundary

Gate V2 requires each of `P`, `W`, and `A` to be 15–60% of all eligible positives in each history. The proposal's `[12.5%, 62.5%]` band is an explicitly provisional ±2.5 percentage-point engineering tolerance, not a derived statistical band. Within that proposal, the new soft regions would be `12.5–<15%` and `>60–62.5%`. Those values violate the unchanged Gate V2 hard mix predicate. Treating them as soft under this design would silently amend the inherited support contract, which Sprint 19 is not authorized to do. Values outside the proposed band also remain hard failures under the proposal. Accordingly, Task 3 should omit the expanded band as an operative soft category and retain the existing 15–60% hard constraint wherever the inherited contract applies. Any separately authorized future exploratory contract that proposes different mix limits must state the change and support rationale prospectively; this note does not approve one.

Mix is calculated over all eligible positives. A permitted excursion could alter the P/W composition of the primary event set within a history and so change the mix of score comparisons; `A` affects the denominator even though it is not a primary event. Equal weighting of history AUROCs does not remove this within-history composition effect. The band does not estimate real-world prevalence and cannot establish robustness to a support shift. The proposal counts one mix miss per affected cohort, not one per history; multiple cohort deviations on one history are separate misses and exceed its proposed one-miss-per-history allowance.

The proposal's historical Sprint 18 example (`A=20/135=14.81%`) retains its original failed verdict. The new band cannot reclassify that history, rescue any old candidate, or tune a future threshold from old outcomes. The Sprint 18 Tasks 64/65 records are relevant only for their documented contract distinction and unresolved rationale—not as threshold-calibration data.

## Construction support: 48 versus 32 versus 25

The operative Sprint 18 construction reserve is `48` deterministic selected negative-control windows per history under the existing construction contract. The inherited per-history hard control floor is `25`. The Design promotion margin is `32`, derived arithmetically as `ceil(25 × 1.25) = 32`. They answer different questions: 48 is a construction-time reserve/reject-on-shortfall rule, 32 is a Design promotion target, and 25 is a hard qualification floor. Neither `32` nor `25` is an approved replacement construction quota.

For Task 1, the control windows enter the denominator of both primary AUROCs, so fewer controls can reduce the available within-history comparisons. But the number of selected windows is not the number of independent histories. Even when control windows are non-overlapping, pairwise AUROC terms reuse event and control scores from the same history; robot-bound support and shared score inputs do not turn those terms into independent observations. The records provide no justified effective-support model, uncertainty target, power/precision criterion, or fully qualified prospective comparison showing that a quota of 32 or 25 preserves the estimand's adequacy. Conversely, the construction and historical feasibility rationale for 48 does not establish that the extra 16 over the Design target or 23 over the hard floor are statistically necessary or optimal.

The reviewed Sprint 18 Tasks 64/65 leave both propositions unresolved: 48 is the operative contract, not a proved statistical necessity; 32/25 are not established safe construction reductions. Old diagnostic counts or pass/fail outcomes must not select a lower quota or revise any old verdict. Therefore the exploratory construction quota remains **unresolved**. Task 3 should mark it as a prospective prerequisite, retain 48 for any Sprint 18 work, and not silently substitute 32 or 25.

## Draft input and evidence needed

For Task 3, carry forward these boundaries:

- Keep Task 1's separate `S_pred` and `S_pop` P+W history-macro endpoints, common support, paired history bootstrap, complete roster, and `UNCOMPUTABLE` handling unchanged.
- Preserve every inherited hard support, provenance, role-isolation, chronology, concentration, lead-support, and mix predicate. No hard failure, missing required check, or unusable history can be counted as a soft miss or rescued by pooling.
- Do not encode `H=32`, a two-miss operating budget, any Design-margin miss allowance, or the `12.5–62.5%` band as approved decision thresholds. The margin thresholds may appear as descriptive per-history tags; the expanded mix band should be omitted under the present contract.
- Leave the appropriate Evaluation-history count, any soft disposition, and the exploratory construction quota explicitly unresolved. If future evidence supports alternatives, require a separately authorized version and independent review before outcomes; do not tune from Sprint 18 results.
- Keep exploratory usability separate from Gate V2 `MEASURABLE`, and preserve the stop on data contact, training, Confirmation/Sealed use, or Sprint 18 continuation.

A defensible future numerical allowance would need a named history-level decision or precision objective and a prospective sampling regime; an explicit model for between-history variation and dependence of score/support predicates; and pre-outcome sensitivity or simulation across candidate support/mix rules that assesses the two primary estimates and their uncertainty without treating windows or pair terms as independent histories. A construction-quota proposal additionally needs evidence, under a separately authorized prospective contract, that the selected controls support the intended score and any retained false-alert estimand. Those prerequisites are absent here; no new outcome data were accessed for this analysis.
