# Sprint 18 Full-Training Representation Protocol

**Status:** DRAFT — prospectively freeze in Sprint 18 Task 1 before any Sprint 18 data or model outcome is inspected.
**Scope:** Eight-arm, full-training comparison of Sprint 17 B0/K3/K7/K9 and four new self-supervised mechanisms.

## 1. Scientific question

Sprint 17 trained every trainable arm for 300 optimizer steps (about 7.5 epochs on 5,040 Fit files at batch size 128). Its recorded contrastive coefficient was `0.0` throughout because the absolute 980-step warmup exceeded the complete run. Sprint 18 asks two bounded questions:

1. Do the relative gains of K3, K7, and K9 over B0 persist when every trainable arm receives a schedule long enough to train prediction and contrastive objectives?
2. Does one of four prospectively specified mechanisms improve representation or scoring beyond a fully trained B0 under the same data, seeds, schedule, and evaluation contract?

Sprint 18 does not claim that more training must improve AUROC, that Sprint 17 found a causal bottleneck, or that any arm is production-ready.

## 3. Data and information boundaries

- Use one fresh, collision-free Sprint 18 role binding generated by the accepted Sprint 15 Candidate 7 methodology: 4 Design histories, 3 Fit histories, 1 Calibration history, 4 Development histories, and 4 Confirmation histories.
- Preflight the complete roster once. No reserve roster, per-history replacement, retry, omission, pooling, or favorable seed selection.
- Design is for structural checks, runtime feasibility, and healthy-only balance measurement. Coefficient pilots train only on Fit healthy rows and evaluate their balance/retention gates only on Design healthy rows; Design is never added to final representation fitting.
- Fit contains verified healthy rows used for representation fitting, EMA targets, reference banks, and Fit-only auxiliary calibrators.
- Calibration sets per-arm q95 operating thresholds only.
- Development reports all eight arms and learning curves; it does not select which valid arms reach Confirmation.
- Confirmation is untouched until all eight final configs, checkpoints, banks, calibrators, metric code, and hashes are locked.
- Sprint 15 Sealed histories remain forbidden to enumerate, open, hash, probe, score, or use.
- Labels, cohort, subtype, severity, failure time, anomaly masks, simulator hidden state, Development outcomes, and Confirmation outcomes are never training inputs or coefficient-selection inputs.

A fresh Sprint 18 benchmark must pass the inherited structural and observable measurability gates without silently inheriting Sprint 17's Design-only waiver. Any waiver requires an explicit reviewed protocol amendment and exact qualified language.

## 4. Common full-training contract

### 4.1 Proposed schedule to freeze

Task 1 must either freeze these values verbatim or record a prospective amendment before any execution:

| Setting | Value |
|---|---:|
| model seeds | 3 new fixed seeds |
| epochs | 100 complete Fit epochs |
| batch size | 128 files |
| optimizer | AdamW |
| initial learning rate | `1.5e-3` |
| weight decay | `0.0` for representation arms |
| gradient clipping | global norm `1.0` |
| precision | float32 |
| LR warmup | 5 epochs, linear from `0.1 × lr` to `lr` |
| LR decay | cosine from epoch 5 through epoch 100 |
| minimum LR | `1e-5` |
| EMA decay | `0.996` |
| total mask ratio | `0.40` unless the arm spec allocates that same total differently |
| contrastive warmup | first 10 complete epochs, coefficient exactly zero |
| contrastive ramp | epochs 11–30, linear |
| full-coefficient plateau | epochs 31–100 |
| primary checkpoint | final optimizer state after epoch 100 |

The exact optimizer-step count is `100 × ceil(number_of_Fit_files / 128)` and must be recorded. An epoch visits each eligible Fit file exactly once under the deterministic sampler. Do not hard-code the Sprint 17 value of 40 steps/epoch when the fresh Fit support differs.

There is no AUROC-based early stopping and no best-checkpoint search. Save diagnostic checkpoints after step 300 and epochs 10, 30, 60, and 100. Development learning curves are descriptive; the epoch-100 checkpoint is the sole primary checkpoint and the only checkpoint eligible for Confirmation.

### 4.2 Loss-coefficient balance

The purpose is to make every declared auxiliary objective active without allowing it to dominate prediction. Coefficients are frozen using Design/Fit healthy data before Development outcomes.

For a shared-encoder parameter set `theta`, measure

`r_aux = ||grad_theta(weight × L_aux)||_2 / (||grad_theta(L_pred)||_2 + 1e-12)`.

Measure without changing optimizer state every 40 optimizer steps over the last five epochs of the coefficient ramp. A coefficient candidate is balance-valid only when all model seeds satisfy:

- all losses and gradients finite;
- median `r_aux` in `[0.15, 0.50]`;
- 90th percentile `r_aux <= 1.0`;
- median per-dimension file-embedding standard deviation `>= 0.05`;
- held-out Design healthy prediction loss no more than `1.10 ×` the matched prediction-only pilot.

Candidate grids:

- B0/K3/K7/K9 InfoNCE `lambda_max`: `[0.025, 0.05, 0.10]`;
- N1-HCC `lambda_hcc_max`: `[0.025, 0.05, 0.10]`;
- N2-MSP coarse coefficient `alpha`: `[0.10, 0.25, 0.50]`;
- N3-CCRP relational coefficient `beta`: `[0.10, 0.25, 0.50]`.

Choose the smallest candidate satisfying every balance condition. Select one common InfoNCE coefficient on `B0-FULL`, then copy it unchanged to `B0-FULL`, `K3-FULL`, `K7-FULL`, `K9-FULL`, `N2-MSP`, and `N3-CCRP`. N1 selects only its HCC coefficient. After the common InfoNCE coefficient is frozen, N2 and N3 select only their declared auxiliary coefficient with InfoNCE active. If no candidate is valid, stop that path and amend the protocol prospectively; do not widen the grid after reading Development or Confirmation outcomes.

N2 and N3 retain the common active InfoNCE coefficient in addition to their auxiliary loss. N1 replaces InfoNCE rather than adding a second file-level contrastive loss. N4 adds no representation loss.

## 5. Optimization and checkpoint invariants

- Every trainable arm starts from a fresh initialization under the same three model seeds. No Sprint 17 weights are warm-started.
- Save model, EMA target, optimizer, LR scheduler, global step, epoch, contrastive schedule state, RNG states, and config together.
- Resume only from an internally coherent checkpoint. A partial restore is invalid.
- Fit reference banks and score standardizers are built from the arm's final Fit state only.
- A restored bank is used as-is unless the protocol explicitly calls for a Fit refit; Development and Confirmation never refit.
- Record prediction loss, raw auxiliary loss, weighted auxiliary contribution, effective coefficient, both gradient norms, embedding variance, and valid support at least once per epoch.
- Parameter and reference-sample FLOP deltas are reported against `B0-FULL`; an envelope must be frozen before execution. No hidden width/depth increase may rescue an arm.

### 5.1 Proposed compute envelopes to freeze

- K3/K7/K9 retain the Sprint 17 inference envelope: parameters and reference-sample FLOPs each `<= 1.10 × B0-FULL`.
- N1 primary inference parameters/FLOPs equal B0; training-only relation-matrix overhead is reported separately.
- N2 parameters `<= 1.10 × B0`; primary fine-score inference `<= 1.10 × B0`; training FLOPs `<= 1.25 × B0`.
- N3 parameters `<= 1.10 × B0`; primary uncorrupted inference `<= 1.10 × B0`; the declared auxiliary second forward permits training FLOPs `<= 2.25 × B0`.
- N4 representation parameters/FLOPs and `S_pop` equal B0; the scale model has at most 100,000 parameters and total scoring FLOPs `<= 1.10 × B0` scoring FLOPs.

Task 1 freezes the measurement input, tooling, and rounding. Exceeding an envelope invalidates the arm; do not shrink a declared mechanism after measurement.

## 6. Scoring and comparisons

- Preserve independent `S_pred` and `S_pop`; no fusion or outcome-selected branch switching.
- Primary estimand remains tie-aware event P+W macro AUROC over common per-history support.
- Report P, W, A companion, per-history and per-seed values, 95% history-block LCB, directional count, recall/lead, severity ordering, localization, subtype slices, FAR, and unaffected-background stability.
- Report background point estimate and its uncertainty explicitly; do not imply that a per-seed CI is an aggregate gate CI.
- `K3-FULL`, `K7-FULL`, `K9-FULL`, and N1–N4 are paired against `B0-FULL` on identical support.
- For learning dynamics, Development also reports every saved checkpoint, but only epoch 100 is eligible for a recovery claim.
- N2 and N3 auxiliary residuals are diagnostics only; they do not replace or fuse into primary `S_pred` in Sprint 18.
- N4 changes `S_pred` by its declared normalization and leaves `S_pop` bitwise equal to `B0-FULL` on identical cached embeddings.

Task 1 must freeze minimum-effect, P/W non-inferiority, nuisance, background, compute, and recovery gates before outcomes. Unless prospectively amended, inherit Sprint 17's `Delta_PW >= +0.05`, `Delta_P >= -0.02`, `Delta_W >= -0.02`, FAR limits, and per-history background ratio `<= 0.10`.

## 7. Confirmation and verdict

All eight valid arms proceed to one-shot Confirmation. Development ranking cannot remove an arm, tune a coefficient, alter a mask allocation, change a checkpoint, or choose a score branch.

Allowed final verdicts:

- `FULL_TRAINING_RECOVERY`: one or more old-stack arms pass every frozen recovery gate against `B0-FULL`;
- `NEW_MECHANISM_RECOVERY`: one or more N1–N4 arms pass every frozen recovery gate;
- `MIXED_RECOVERY`: at least one old-stack and one new arm pass;
- `NO_REPRODUCIBLE_RECOVERY`: all arms are validly evaluated but none pass;
- `UNRESOLVED`: a prospectively defined invalidity or incompleteness trigger prevents the bounded comparison.

A relative arm win does not establish that more training caused the win, that Sprint 17's short schedule was wrong, or that a component is causally defective. No result authorizes production, calibrated-risk, fleet-prevalence, or sealed-generalization claims.
