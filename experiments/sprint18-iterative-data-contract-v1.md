## 4. One selected repair: extend exposure at unchanged per-time physics

**Selected mechanism:** keep the C2 rate amendment as historical input (`A abrupt_rate=3.3e-5`) and extend calendar exposure from 360 to **450 days** while preserving the v7/C2 per-time operation cadence, rates, health dynamics, routes, fleet, maintenance, reset, quarantine, and event predicates. This increases observation time rather than packing more operations into the existing 360-day span. The calendar validator's current 400-day upper bound must be prospectively expanded to 450 for this profile. The chosen configuration delta from `sprint18_c2_history_config(seed)` is exactly:

| Leaf | C2 base | Iterative v1 | Rule |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| `factory.span_days` | 360 | 450 | +25% elapsed calendar exposure. |
| `factory.dev_cutoff_days` | 180 | 225 | Retain the v7 50/50 chronological split. |
| `scheduler.n_units` | 2304 | 2880 | +25% arrivals at the same `arrival_interval_s` and `arrival_jitter_s`; do not rescale either cadence value. |
| `factory.quarantine_days` | 7 | 7 | Unchanged. |
| all health/rate/physics/signal/fleet/route/maintenance/eligibility fields | C2 | byte/value equivalent | Unchanged; assert the complete resolved-config diff contains only the three leaves above. |

The new factory entry is `sprint18_iterative_v1_history_config(seed)`, additively composed from `sprint18_c2_history_config(seed)`. It uses source/CLI profile `sprint18-iterative-v1`; the chronicle manifest `protocol` remains `sprint15-benchmark-protocol-v7`. The iterative contract ID is recorded in the candidate binding and attempt record, which bind the manifest/config/source hashes; do not replace the inherited v7 manifest protocol with the data-contract ID. Do not use CLI `--units`: its existing behavior rescales cadence to preserve span, the opposite of this repair. Preserve the manifest's Sprint 15 allocation block as absent for Sprint 18 roles, as in the accepted Sprint 18 materialization contract; allocation evidence is separately computed and bound by the prospective candidate/preflight record.

The 25% exposure increase is a **method hypothesis, not a guarantee**. At constant per-time opportunity it would project 10 eligible subtype events to 12.5 and 46 controls to 57.5; those are arithmetic illustrations only, not expected-value claims or acceptance evidence. Longer histories may create feedback through degradation, failure/reset overlap, censoring, mix, and lead support. Actual exact allocation and every independent qualification gate must pass on the reviewed fixture and later on all bound roles. If they do not, preserve the failure; do not tune rates, move labels, lower gates, expand the calendar beyond 450, or select a seed. Any next method change is a newly versioned and independently reviewed correction under the same success-only loop.

Why this is the smallest defensible direction: C1's A-mix miss motivated the already-consumed C2 rate change; C2/C3 show that changing that rate alone left hard control/subtype failures. Increasing calendar time at unchanged hazard and arrival cadence can add eligible events and control opportunities without increasing event density per day or rewriting the physical mechanisms. Candidate 5's v5 change did the opposite—1.5× more arrivals at fixed span—and controls collapsed to 13/11; this contract expressly does not repeat that density change. Lowering event quotas/caps/spacing, shrinking the window, changing reset/quarantine/lead rules, relabeling subtypes, or lowering any gate is rejected.
